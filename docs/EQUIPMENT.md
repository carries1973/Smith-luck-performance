# Smith Luck Performance — Home-Gym Equipment

## Programming constraint

Programming must use equipment confirmed in Ryan's home gym. Do not prescribe a commercial-machine-only movement without offering an approved home-gym substitute.

## Photo-confirmed inventory

### Strength

- Power rack with multi-grip pull-up station
- Olympic barbells and plates
- Adjustable/flat bench
- Dumbbells and kettlebells
- Chains and specialty-bar attachment

### Cable and bodyweight

- Lat-pulldown/cable tower with low-cable options
- Resistance bands in multiple strengths
- Suspension straps
- Pull-up and dip options

### Conditioning

- Indoor spin bike
- Treadmill
- Rowing machine
- Heavy bag and freestanding bag
- Medicine balls and jump ropes

### Recovery and mobility

- Far-infrared sauna
- BOSU and stability ball
- Exercise mat and foam roller
- Mobility and balance accessories

## Known exclusion

- No dedicated hamstring-curl machine is assumed.

Posterior-chain alternatives may use Romanian deadlifts, supported single-leg hinges, hip thrusts, band curls, cable variations, sliding curls or other confirmed-equipment options appropriate to the program.

## Maintenance rule

New equipment is not considered available until Ryan confirms it. Record additions or removals here before programming depends on them.
