# Smith Luck Performance — Product Brief

## Product purpose

Smith Luck Performance is Ryan's personal home-gym training journal and coaching app. It must make the correct workout easy to start, guide the session, record every working set or interval, preserve history, and adapt future recommendations without losing Ryan's goals or the Smith Luck identity.

The product is a working training surface, not a marketing website.

## Primary user and intent

- Primary user: Ryan, age 54.
- Primary goals: add visible muscle, maintain or improve strength, improve cardiovascular fitness, remain athletic and durable for snowboarding beyond age 55, and support longevity and recovery.
- Training context: home gym, indoor cycling preferred, fatigue and stress must influence daily recommendations.
- Method: conjugate principles. Primary and dynamic variations rotate while movement patterns and long-term goals stay stable.

## Core experience

1. Open the dashboard and see the correct next recommended session.
2. Choose or resume a workout without day-identity mismatch.
3. Complete a readiness check before training.
4. Start one session timer.
5. Follow a consistent workout interface across all seven days.
6. Record every prescribed set separately: load, reps, RPE and completion.
7. Use exercise rest timers or the conditioning interval timer.
8. Record exercise-specific and session-wide notes near the relevant work.
9. Finish as completed, modified or ended early.
10. Review history and previous performance.

## Weekly programming foundation

1. Upper Strength
2. Lower Strength
3. Indoor Cycling + Conditioning
4. Recovery
5. Upper Dynamic / Hypertrophy
6. Lower Dynamic / Hypertrophy
7. Rest / Flexible Recovery

The exact recommendation may move based on readiness, missed sessions and recovery, but displayed day, selected day and stored session must always agree.

## Non-negotiable product rules

- Preserve the established Smith Luck brand and premium dark-green palette.
- Use one shared workout-day component system; programming data changes, controls do not.
- Store individual sets, not one load for an entire exercise.
- Never assume commercial-gym machines.
- Do not silently change goals, cycle structure or training priorities.
- Explain coaching adjustments and keep Ryan in control.
- Training guidance is not medical diagnosis or treatment.
- Supabase becomes the persistent source of truth only after the workout UX is verified.

## Definition of a live usable app

The app is considered live-ready when authenticated workout data persists across devices, incomplete sessions resume reliably, timers work through normal phone use, programming can rotate by cycle, history drives recommendations, failures are recoverable, privacy controls are in place, and the complete iPhone workflow has passed real-session testing.
